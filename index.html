Mission

Our club creates engaging, English-friendly, and student-led activities that bring Vietnamese and international BUV students together through shared cultural experiences, social interaction, and peer connection. We aim to:

1

Create recurring events where local and international students interact meaningfully through mixed teams, games, cultural sharing, workshops, and collaborative activities.

2

Work with Student Engagement and other BUV stakeholders to co-host or promote inclusive activities that are accessible and appealing to international students.

3
